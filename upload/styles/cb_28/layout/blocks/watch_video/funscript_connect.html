Bluetooth

Switch on your device and close other applications connected to it. Select it in the browser Bluetooth dialog.

Intiface supports a wide range of devices, from vibrators to strokers (supported devices). Availability through direct Bluetooth depends on the device.

If your device does not appear or you have trouble connecting, install Intiface Central on your desktop and use the Intiface connection.

Bluetooth requires HTTPS and a browser with Web Bluetooth, such as Chrome or Edge. Otherwise use Intiface.
